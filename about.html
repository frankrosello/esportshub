ABOUT ESPORTSHUB

The Work Around
the Game, Together.

An esports team runs on more than its match schedule. EsportsHub brings the preparation, people, and follow-through into one shared hub.

HOW TO GET STARTED

From Invite to Match Day

  1. Create your account. Sign up, then complete your player profile.
  2. Join your team. Accept an invitation or ask an administrator to assign your team and role.
  3. Set up your week. Share availability, choose your default team, and review upcoming events.
  4. Keep improving. Prepare for each session and leave useful notes after it.

A Few Things to Know

Can I join without an invitation?

You can create an account, but team access needs an invitation or an administrator assignment. Signing up does not grant staff permissions.

Does it replace NECC check-in?

Captains still check in through NECC. EsportsHub keeps the match link alongside your team’s schedule and preparation details.

Can friends and family follow a team?

Yes. Guest invitations provide access to supported team information without exposing private notes or concerns.

Where are Workshop logs analyzed?

Supported logs are analyzed locally in your browser. The raw log is not uploaded. Sharing an analysis summary as a team report is a separate action.

Does it need Discord?

No. The core scheduling, team coordination, reports, and in-site notifications work without a Discord bot.
