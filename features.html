THE TOOLS

A Better Week.
A More Prepared Team.

Bring the work before and after each game into the same place. Start with the schedule; keep the details connected.

01 / PLAN

Find a Time That Works

Share one weekly availability schedule across your teams. Compare half-hour windows, see which starters can play, and use ranked practice suggestions to choose your next session.

  • Shared weekly availability
  • Team coverage by time slot
  • Suggested practice windows
02 / PREPARE

Know the Plan Before You Play

Keep events, RSVPs, starting lineups, substitutes, and warmup details together. Open the NECC match link for official captain check-in; configured teams can sync their NECC schedules and results.

  • Team schedules and event details
  • Event RSVPs and lineups
  • Attendance and match readiness
03 / REVIEW

Keep the Takeaways Useful

Write formatted team reports with map names and replay codes. Attach supporting links and media. Analyze supported Overwatch Workshop logs locally in your browser and publish the summary when you choose.

  • Scrim and VOD reports
  • Map and replay-code blocks
  • Supported Workshop log analysis
04 / CONNECT

Give Everyone the Right Context

Manage team rosters and role assignments, share news with supporters, and customize your profile. Private concerns have a separate conversation flow for their author and authorized game staff.

  • Team news and guest views
  • Role-based access and invitations
  • Profiles and private concerns
