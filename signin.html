A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before you jump in.

How do I get access to a team?

Create an account, then accept a team invitation or ask an administrator to assign your team and role. Creating an account does not grant staff permissions.

Can friends and family use EsportsHub?

Yes. Assigned players can create guest invitations. Guests can follow team dashboards, news, events, and public rosters without accessing private notes or concerns.

Does EsportsHub replace NECC check-in?

No. Captains still check in through NECC. EsportsHub provides the match link and brings schedules, preparation, and configured result syncing together.

What happens to my Workshop log?

Supported Workshop logs are analyzed locally in your browser. The raw log is not uploaded. A summary is shared with your team only if you publish it as a note.

Who can read player concerns?

The author and authorized game managers or administrators. Unrelated teammates, guests, captains, and coaches do not automatically receive access.

Workshop Log Analysis

Import a ScrimTime Workshop Inspector log to review map and player statistics inside EsportsHub.

Import a Log

In Overwatch, enable the Workshop Inspector log file and host your custom game with ScrimTime code Z0ASA. Import the exported text file from Documents/Overwatch/Workshop. This is EsportsHub’s local analyzer, not the Parsertime service or its skill-rating system.

Logs are processed locally in your browser. Only a note summary is shared if you choose to publish it.
