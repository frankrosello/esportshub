FOR TEAMS

One Team.
Different Perspectives.

Less noise, more context. Assigned roles shape what you can see and manage in the club hub.

Players & Substitutes

Keep your next session in focus.

  • Submit availability once across your teams.
  • Respond to events and check your lineup assignment.
  • Review team notes, map pools, news, and match information.

Captains & Coaches

Turn preparation into a team habit.

  • Coordinate lineups, substitutes, warmups, and attendance.
  • Review shared availability to plan practice.
  • Keep coaching takeaways in team reports.

Team & Game Managers

Keep the operation moving.

  • Manage the teams and games assigned to you.
  • Maintain resources and configured NECC schedule/result syncing.
  • Game managers can update map pools and review authorized private concerns.

Administrators

Keep access and organization clear.

  • Manage games, teams, accounts, roles, and invitations.
  • Review organization activity across accessible teams.
  • Control team access without sharing staff privileges.

Guests & Supporters

Follow the team from the sidelines.

  • Use a guest invitation to join the community.
  • Follow accessible team events, news, results, and public rosters.
  • Private team reports and player concerns stay restricted.

Access depends on your actual assignments. Switching your view changes the interface; it does not grant additional permissions.
